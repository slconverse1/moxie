# HELP

Moving through the site (GC-free moxie).

## What the history holds

The site keeps a history of the pages you visit, for the Introduction, the Mixer’s Guide, and the Spec (including the reference and its function pages). Each page is added when you arrive at it, by a link or by the arrows below. The history holds the last 50 pages and is kept apart from the moxie/gc site’s history. Your place on each page is remembered, so you come back to where you were reading.

The Tutorial, Style, Try, REPL and Help pages are not added.

## Moving back and forth

- **Left and right arrow keys** step one page back or forward, with a short note of where you are in the history. Hold a key and the pages flow by until you let go.
- **A swipe** (on a touch screen) moves through up to ten pages, each shown in full as it passes. Swipe again and it carries on with the next ten, without replaying pages it has already shown.
- **Tap, click or press any key** while pages are moving to stop on the page in view. That page opens in full and becomes the current page.
