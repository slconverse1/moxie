# HELP

Moving through the site (GC-free moxie).

## What the history holds

The site keeps a history of the pages you visit in the Introduction, the Mixer’s Guide, and the Spec (including the reference and its function pages). A page is added when you arrive at it by following a link. The history holds up to 12 pages; when it is full, the oldest page drops off the start, and it is kept apart from the moxie/gc site’s history. Your place on each page is remembered, so you come back to where you were reading.

Moving back and forward through the history adds nothing, and a page you stop at is not added just for being stopped at. But if you then leave it by any link that takes you to another page (a link in the text, a Contents link, or a tab in the menu bar), the page is added again, because it led somewhere. A new page in the history follows it.

The Tutorial, Style, Try, REPL and Help pages are not part of the history.

## Moving back and forth

- **Left and right arrow keys** step one page back or forward, with a short note of where you are in the history. Hold a key and the pages flow by until you let go.
- **A short swipe** (on a touch screen) shows the next page, back or forward.
- **A long swipe** sets the pages flowing by, each shown in full, as many as ten: the longer the swipe, the more pages. Swipe again and it carries on with the next ten, without replaying pages it has already shown.
- **Tap, click or press any key** while pages are moving to stop on the page in view. That page opens in full and becomes the current page.
